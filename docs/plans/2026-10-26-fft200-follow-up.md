## Locked design

Compare FFT199, FFT200, repaired KL and bucketed KL. Keep source 322bd1f,
chunk 8, block 32, precision, first passage and normal cache behavior fixed.
First verify that the observation wrapper reproduces the uninstrumented API
and blocked full-horizon control. Only uninstrumented objective calls are timed.

Behavioral pilot: 4,096 trials per condition and method for sig 5,10,15 crossed
with ordinary, quarter-strength and zero drift, and cr 5/20 (crsd 2). Include
the archived start and fitted vector: 17 cases total. Use separate method noise
streams; equal seed labels do not imply matched FFT draws. Record RT, region,
crossing flag, step, position and generated work. For zero drift, freeze the
ordinary-drift geographic mask and identify it explicitly. Conditional RT
quantiles exclude non-crossings; production category T still includes fallback
responses, and non-crossing probability is reported separately.

Inferential family: all 17 cases, both conditions, three candidate/reference
contrasts, five region probabilities, low-support cutoffs 5/10/20%, no-crossing
probability, 25 conditional RT quantiles and spatial SW1. Allocate family alpha
0.025 to binary/RT endpoints and 0.025 to spatial endpoints using Bonferroni.
Binary differences combine exact Clopper-Pearson bounds, and RT differences
combine binomial order-statistic quantile intervals, with the additional
two-distribution allocation. Sparse intervals remain unbounded/inconclusive.

Before sampling this study, replace the originally proposed spatial bootstrap
with a conservative analytic bound. The 64-direction empirical SW1 is convex,
so its expectation is at least population SW1. Replacing one observation
changes the statistic by at most D/n, where D = 2*sqrt(159^2+99^2) display pixels.
McDiarmid's lower-tail inequality therefore gives the one-sided population
upper bound `observed_SW1 + D*sqrt(0.5*(1/nA+1/nB)*log(1/alpha))`, conditional on
the observed crossing counts. This avoids bootstrap calibration at a zero
distance and is more conservative. The projection directions and grid are fixed.

Choose a fixed qualifying sample size using only the independent pilot:
20 seeds, at least 9,000 trials per seed, at most 45,000. The planner targets
90% central power at equality using conservative marginal-variance estimates,
1.5x inflated RT-density and spatial pilot scaling estimates. This planning
calculation is approximate; final confidence bounds do not rely on it. Sparse
pilot conditional groups (<50 responses), a sample requirement above the
ceiling or an eight-hour behavioral cap produces an inconclusive gate. Do not
increase sampling based on qualifying outcomes or discard difficult endpoints.
Only the FFT pair advances to costly qualification; KL pilot differences remain
diagnostic and cannot be called equivalent. Family allocation is unchanged.

Replay the first 30 valid evaluated vectors in the archived simplex trace,
in their original order. Each arm has three warm-up calls, a separately recorded
first replay, and 30 timed complete replays. Alternate forward/reverse method
order, clear the two-entry host cache at replay start only, and synchronize each
objective. Use whole-replay paired bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples).
The speed gate requires >=10% lower median replay cost and an upper 95% ratio
bound below one versus FFT199; report KL ratios separately. Twelve-hour soft
timing cap. Save every objective duration/loss and GPU telemetry every 5 seconds.
Telemetry memory readings are sampled, not a guaranteed allocator peak.

## Gated matched fits

Launch only if FFT200 passes both behavioral and replay gates. Generate one
9,000-trial-per-condition synthetic dataset with FFT199. All four methods use
that same data and the same 20% perturbed truth start, training seed 321,
unchanged Nelder-Mead, 1,000 iterations and 135-minute training cap. Save all
evaluations and best-so-far checkpoints every five training minutes. Record
warm-up time separately and include it in reported time to quality. Preserve
capped and failed runs; refuse silent continuation of an interrupted fit trace.

Before fitting, calibrate exploratory predictive limits from 20 independent
FFT truth replicates at the validation sample size, compared with a separate
180,000-trial reference. Keep this finite calibration's limitations explicit.
Score every checkpoint on 20 fresh seeds through the same original-FFT scorer;
the score tests parameter predictions under the reference law, not native KL
equivalence. Assess native simulator fidelity separately in the earlier stage.
Use maximum region/non-crossing probability error, maximum conditional RT
quantile error and spatial SW1; preserve missing values as failures to qualify.
Validation cap: eight hours of scoring (excluding training time).

Recovery screen: mean active relative error <=5%, worst <=10%, with inactive sv
retained and reported separately. Time to the first acceptable checkpoint
includes warm-up, training and cumulative verification cost. With one matched
case, report descriptive differences only: no successful-fit-rate inference or
general end-to-end speedup qualification. Do not change production defaults.

Qualification and fit runs are conditional, so elapsed cost is not known until
the pilot. Trace replay alone was estimated at several hours from the screen.
All caps are soft and checked between GPU calls; preserve partial results.
