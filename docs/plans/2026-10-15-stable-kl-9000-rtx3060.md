## Repair

The existing ranked Fourier basis and eigenvalues are unchanged. Each retained complex Fourier mode additionally carries its physical flattened grid ID. Gaussian real/imaginary coefficients receive a key folded by that ID before time samples are generated. Eigenvalue rank changes, inserted modes and padding therefore do not reassign coefficients belonging to existing modes.

Both original full-horizon K–L and improvement 1 use `stable_kl_noise=True`. The historical default remains available with the flag off so previous seeded experiments can be reproduced. The two implementations retain different noise layouts across time blocks; their root seeds alone do not imply identical original-versus-early-stop samples. Early stopping is checked exactly against the full-horizon blocked control under the repaired layout.

This repair improves common-random-number coupling; it does not smooth hard choices, threshold crossings, RT-bin counts or changes in the retained mode set. It does not guarantee identifiable parameters or a successful optimizer.
